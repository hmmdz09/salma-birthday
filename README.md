# The Shape of My Heart — Queen of Hearts Edition ✨♠️♥️♦️♣️

An interactive, poetic, and cinematic birthday dedication created for **Salma Aulia**, inspired by the iconic song and guitar melody of **"Shape of My Heart" by Sting**.

## 🃏 Concept & Story: The 4 Suits of Salma
> *"I know that the spades are the swords of a soldier..."*  
> *"I know that the clubs are weapons of war..."*  
> *"I know that diamonds mean money for this art..."*  
> *"But that's not the shape of my heart."*

- ♠️ **The Spades (Resilience)**: Acknowledging Salma's quiet strength, bravery, and persistence in walking forward.
- ♣️ **The Clubs (Laughter & Charm)**: The warmth, humor, and easy comfort of shared smiles.
- ♦️ **The Diamonds (Grace & Authenticity)**: Natural elegance, shining intellect, and true inner beauty.
- ♥️ **The Hearts (The Shape of My Heart)**: Beyond all symbols and games, the only shape of the heart that matters is Salma Aulia.

## ✨ Features
1. **Cinematic Entrance Curtain**: Royal gold frame, Queen of Hearts crest with Salma's portrait, and automatic audio playback on enter.
2. **Gold Vinyl Audio Player**: Minimalist floating player playing Sting's *Shape of My Heart* with synchronized real-time lyrics.
3. **The 3D Deck of Destiny**:
   - 7 fannable luxury playing cards featuring Salma's photos.
   - Smooth 3D card-flip interaction revealing the photo and philosophical reflection.
   - Controls: Fan Out, Flip All, Draw Random Card, and Upload New Photo Cards directly.
4. **The Golden Flame Lantern**: Pejamkan mata, make a wish, and blow the golden candles to trigger golden smoke and royal fireworks.
5. **The Royal Wax-Sealed Letter**: 3D envelope with golden `H ♥ S` seal opening to reveal a deeply personal birthday letter.
6. **Celestial Constellation Board**: Interactive wishes board where visitors can leave prayers with playing card emblems.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **HTML5 & Vanilla CSS3**: 3D Transforms, Glassmorphism, Gold Foil Gradients, Keyframe Physics.
- **JavaScript (ES6+)**: Web Audio API Chime & Wind Synthesis, Canvas Confetti, Starfield & Drifting Suits Canvas.
- **Music**: *Shape of My Heart* (Sting & Dominic Miller).
